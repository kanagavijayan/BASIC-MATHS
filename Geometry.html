Geometry

Geometry is the branch of mathematics that studies shapes, sizes, positions, and properties of space. It begins with simple ideas like points, lines, and angles, and extends to complex structures such as polygons, circles, and 3D solids

🔹 Core Elements of Geometry

  • Point: A location in space with no size, represented by a dot.
  • Line: Extends infinitely in both directions, made of points.
  • Line Segment: A part of a line with two endpoints.
  • Ray: Starts at one point and extends infinitely in one direction.
  • Angle: Formed by two rays sharing a common endpoint (vertex).
  • Plane: A flat, two-dimensional surface extending infinitely.

🔹 Types of Lines

  • Parallel Lines: Lines in the same plane that never meet (e.g., railway tracks).
  • Intersecting Lines: Lines that cross at a point.
  • Perpendicular Lines: Lines that meet at a right angle (90°).

🔹 Shapes and Figures

  • Polygons: Closed shapes with straight sides (triangles, squares, pentagons).
  • Circles: All points equidistant from a center.
  • 3D Solids: Cubes, spheres, cones, cylinders, etc.

🔹 Applications of Geometry

  • Architecture & Engineering: Designing buildings, bridges, and machines.
  • Art & Design: Creating patterns, symmetry, and perspective.
  • Navigation & Mapping: Using coordinates and angles for directions.
  • Technology: Computer graphics, robotics, and 3D modeling.
