Calculus

Calculus is the branch of mathematics that studies change and accumulation. It has two main parts: Differential Calculus (rates of change, slopes, derivatives) and Integral Calculus (areas, accumulation, totals). Together, they help us understand motion, growth, and patterns in science, engineering, and everyday life.

📊 What Statistics Is

  • Definition: The science of data — organizing, analyzing, and interpreting numerical information.
  • Applications: Used in weather forecasting, stock market analysis, medical research, quality control, and social sciences.
  • Population vs Sample: A population is the entire group being studied, while a sample is a smaller subset used to make inferences.

🔑 Types of Statistics

  • Descriptive statistics:
    *Summarizes data using measures like mean, median, mode.
    * Shows variability with range, variance, standard deviation.
    * Often visualized with graphs, charts, and tables.
  • Inferential statistics:
    * Uses probability theory to make predictions or generalizations about a population based on sample data.
    * Includes hypothesis testing, confidence intervals, and regression analysis

📐 Key Concepts

  • Mean: The average of all values.
  • Median: The middle value when data is ordered.
  • Mode: The most frequently occurring value.
  • Standard deviation: Measures how spread out values are.
  • Probability: The foundation of inferential statistics, dealing with uncertainty.
