Mathematics

Mathematics is both a language and a tool — it’s how we describe patterns, relationships, and structures in the world. At its core, maths is about logic and problem-solving, but it branches into many fascinating areas:

  • Arithmetic: The foundation — addition, subtraction, multiplication, division. It’s the maths of everyday life.
  • Geometry: Shapes, sizes, and the properties of space. From ancient pyramids to modern architecture, geometry is everywhere.
  • Algebra: Using symbols to represent numbers and relationships. It’s the bridge between arithmetic and higher mathematics
  • Calculus: The study of change and motion. It powers physics, engineering, and even economics.
  • Statistics: Understanding data, probability, and uncertainty. Essential in science, medicine, and social studies.
  • Number theory: The deep study of integers and prime numbers — surprisingly connected to cryptography and computer security.
